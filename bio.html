Professional Summary

I design and deliver end-to-end BI solutions across Life Insurance, Annuity, SMS, and Fraud domains by translating business requirements into production-ready Tableau dashboards. I build executive and operational reporting for sales performance, distribution trends, new business funnels, underwriting risk, policy lifecycle, claims operations, compliance, and financial leadership reviews. I follow a structured BI pipeline across sourcing, ETL transformation, warehouse loading, and publication to ensure trusted stakeholder reporting.
