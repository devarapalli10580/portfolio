## What Is This Pack?

This directory contains everything you need to walk into an Amazon interview
feeling prepared, confident, and organised. It covers two parallel tracks:

| Track | Files |
|---|---|
| **Behavioural / LP** | `leadership-principles.md`, `star-worksheet.md`, `stories/` |
| **Technical** | `technical-prep.md`, `practice-drills.md` |

Work through both tracks simultaneously — Amazon interviews mix LP and
technical questions in the same loop.

## How to Use This Pack

1. **Read `leadership-principles.md` first.** Understand the six focus LPs, what
   behaviours interviewers are scoring, and the sample question bank.

2. **Fill in `star-worksheet.md`** for each of your real experiences before
   drafting story files. The worksheet forces you to identify metrics early.

3. **Refine each story in `stories/`.** Every story file has a 2-minute
   version (phone screen), an 8-minute version (virtual/on-site), and an
   expandable 15–20-minute outline (deep-dive loop). Practise all three.

4. **Study `technical-prep.md`** section by section. Complete the mini-labs
   and answer the sample questions out loud before checking the outlines.

5. **Run `practice-drills.md` sessions** at least twice — once alone (recording
   yourself) and once with a study partner or mock-interviewer.

6. **Track story usage.** In the table below, tick which story you used per
   interviewer so you never repeat to the same person.

## How to Answer Technical Questions With Structured Thinking

Use the **C-A-R-T** framework for every technical question:

1. **Clarify** — Restate the question and confirm scope ("Just to make sure I
   understand, you're asking about…").
2. **Approach** — Describe your diagnostic or design approach at a high level
   before diving in ("My first step would be to…").
3. **Reason** — Explain *why* you chose each tool or action ("I used traceroute
   here because it shows per-hop latency, which helps isolate…").
4. **Test/Verify** — Describe how you would confirm the solution worked
   ("After the fix, I'd re-run the capture and compare baseline…").

## How to Avoid Repeating Stories Across Interviewers

1. **Before the loop:** List your seven stories on a notepad with one LP tag each.
2. **Between rounds:** Update the Story-Usage Tracker above immediately after
   each interview while the conversation is fresh.
3. **Secondary mapping:** Every story should have a *primary* LP and at least
   one *secondary* LP so you have a fallback.
4. **Pair dissimilar stories to the same LP:** e.g., if you used the ToR-switch
   story for Dive Deep in round 1, use the BTech-ML story for Dive Deep in
   round 2.
5. **Tell the interviewer:** It is completely acceptable to say "I shared a
   network troubleshooting story with an earlier interviewer — let me give
   you a different example that also demonstrates [LP]."
