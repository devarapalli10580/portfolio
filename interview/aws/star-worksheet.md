interview story. Fill in every section before writing the story Markdown file.
Quantify metrics wherever possible — even rough estimates ("roughly 40%",
"about 30 minutes") are better than vague language.

## Common Follow-Up Questions to Prep

- "How did you know you were focusing on the right problem?"
- "What alternatives did you consider and why did you reject them?"
- "What would you do differently if you had more resources?"
- "How did you communicate your approach to stakeholders?"
- "What was the long-term impact 6–12 months later?"
- "How did you validate the root cause before acting?"
